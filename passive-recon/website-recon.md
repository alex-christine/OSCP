# Website Recon

Any locations listed can be plugged into Google Maps or visited depending on the circumstancesIf a client/target has a website that is generally one of the best and simplest places to start.

Small organizations may only have a single website, while large organizations might have many, including some that are not maintained.

Pay attention to:

* Employees
  * Can usually be found on an "About" or "Meet the Team" page
  * May give insight to how corporate emails are structured
  * At least gives some names to look for on other platforms
* Social Media Accounts
  * Corporate accounts will often have pictures helpful for OSINT as they may include:
    * Facilities
    * Badges
    * Employees
* Physical Locations
  * Locations can be plugged into Google Maps or visited depending on the circumstances
* Career Page
  * Or Glassdoor/Linkedin/Monster boards
  * Particularly technical job postings can give a sense of what type of tools they use
    * E.g. if they are hiring a cybersecurity engineer with experience setting up Palo Alto firewalls, it is a fairly safe bet they are (or soon will be) using Palo Alto equipment
